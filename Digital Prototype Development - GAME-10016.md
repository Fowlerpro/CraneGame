**"Crane Game" - Design Documentation**


**Title Story: Sky Salvagers**

**Setting:** Floating above the sky is a platform with a background city added to the theme, you don't control the player that can run on the platform but you can help them to operate a massive grabbing or magnetic crane to help lift the objects so that the players can reach the finish line.

**Story Premise:** You are a rookie at the "Sky Salvagers" that got hired by their company and you believe that a legendary machine could restore the world's technology cause that are getting effected by hackers or viruses that go into the system. The goal is to get to the end and find the legendary machine that can help stop these viruses that spread across the world or going into people system devices.

**Gameplay Twist:**

**Player Control:** The player is control by itself around the 3D space area to help navigate the best crane angle for the object to be interactable with the things that are near the player.

**Crane Control:** Once you are in the game, you can control the crane and extend, rotate, and lowering the grab or magnetic crane to grab the objects.

**Challenge:** Some objects might be really heavy or light depending on how some of them are shape, requiring balance and timing of lifting the objects. What can also be optional is adding sound to the object when you go to lift a object, potentially could set a alarm or a trap indicating that it is too heavy or their is a mine in this object.

**Progression:** When going through the game, some of the objects may grow in bigger or smaller sizes or get faster at respawning which can make the full level of the game difficult to complete once you have made progress halfway through the level.

**Information:** It gives a meaningful opinion both to the movement of the player and the crane operation. This is allow for environmental hazards, having a plan on where the objects are going to be placed once you pick them up when completing the level, and another skill-based grabbing for the grab and magnetic of the crane. Story adds intriguing positive conflict to the situation that they want to stop people doing malicious intension online by hacking on devices and it spreading to our world. This story also teaches us that we need to save the environment and pick these objects that are place in our path so we can have a open field for us to walk in.


**Overview:**

The Crane Game is a 3D endless runner platformer game that shows to work effectively as a player when going through the course. One person controls the crane and the player will keep running in a straight line and not stopping on its position where it spawns, the goal is to get as far as they can with the amount of distance they have and time accordingly to when you have to pick up the objects or when you don't have to pick up the objects depending on where they are randomly spawn to complete this platform course.

**Objective:**

For this game we seek to create an 3D endless runner platformer game that test the player skills to show how efficient they can work and to quickly think fast when picking objects up on the path with one person controlling the crane. For this game project, we mostly want to know is this a engaging platformer game mechanic for players to play and what kind of constraints does it place on level design?

The mechanic for this game allows movement scenarios to create an platformer game that creates a skill based to show how you time yourself efficiently moving the objects out of the way for the player to pass through for our game production. There is also not a lot of games like this that involves you to let the players run straight and you control the crane as your own technical character where the crane is yours to operated and you can move the magnetic pull that is attach to the crane where ever you want to, overall this game is just a special way to perform yourself to show your quick thinking skills and see how far you can get through the level.

**Design Rationale:**

The idea for this game is to make a different or rather compare idea to other games that features this type of mechanics used as it own main or essential mechanic. The Crane Game is an interesting and engaging mechanic, and adding to the limitation for the player only being able to control the crane makes it a difficult challenge to accomplish, which basically forces the player to act quickly in picking the objects up from the ground once they start to spawn more in the game. The player only using a crane and nothing else can help eliminate some challenges that are presented in this game to make it where it is challenge but not as challenge as you think once you start to get the feel of how to play this game. I think this mechanic in particular is a strong level design because it will show from the point where the difficulty of this game will come less from the player to avoid the obstacles that are on the ground but more from controlling the crane and picking up the objects so the player on the ground doesn't run into them but keeps running in a endless straight line. 

The metrics in our game shows the distance on how far they are going from the spawn point to the end point. Usually depending on the objects that spawn they are measure by the amount of weight calculate by a mass that the crane can take to pick them up and how heavy the object is, originally by looking at the weight of the object and the mass of the crane, the crane was able to pickup that object and move it accordingly to it functions. Our game uses the metrics to inform if this object can be lifted or not, but originally we had it where the crane came to grab the object and the object just sink through the ground so we had to fix the collision boxes and the metrics so the crane can grab on to that object and it can be attach together so the object doesn't jump around anywhere or go through the map. It is important that the game is balance and can show a purpose of why this is something important and how you can use these mechanics, we also need to keep in mind why is the player going on a endless straight line and what changes when progressing the level. These considerations around level design and objects placement is a focus on how to make the constraints of the mechanics like a game like this, compare to Crane Simulator or Avoid, the crane itself is the challenge since you got to time your ability on grabbing the objects so the player can pass through while running on a endless path.

This game is engaging beyond a simulation because it shows you real-world scenarios that shows the interaction between being a construction worker doing a job for the community and cleaning up the metal objects that are on the ground. This can create a safe environment for experimentation and learning from both success and failure. This is also effective towards education because it shows students specific learning goals that turn these tools to progress in their skill development.

**The Metrics and Weights for IRL "Crane":**

- ==Crane Configuration:== A lot of cranes can have different types of varying capacity specifications.
- ==Load Radius:== This realizes on the distance between the crane's center and the load's center which can affect the capacity of how much the crane can hold but if you take a larger radius object, this can reduce the lifting capacity.
- ==Counterweights:== This is use to balance the crane and increase the stability of how much can go on a load once being lifted by a crane.
- ==Ground Conditions:== The level of the ground has to be balance or position precisely due to the measurement of capacity of how much can that crane lift if the ground is uneven or unstable.
- ==Weather Conditions:== We also got to look at wind speed and other weather factors that can affect the operation for a crane, so it is mostly best to use a crane when the weather is at a normal level so you are not working in high wind areas.

**Core Gameplay:**

In this game you are using your ability in a creative way to control the crane and move the object from the path road so the player doesn't touch the object. In this game the player will keep running endless on the path and the distance will keep going up on the screen showing how far the player has walk/run, it is the players goal to control the crane and move the objects as quickly as possible before the character hits one of the objects. The player will have to relied on their skills to play this game since it is about quick timing and patience's when playing this game, you don't have to worry about the player running on the path because he is control by a bot which basically means he is uncontrollable and just keeps running on a straight line (just like a NPC character in a game). The goal again for this game is to see how far you can get with Distance, there is no reward for how much distance you have in the game but you can get to see your status of your farthest distance that you reach. So you can also restart the game if you died or hit the objects, those are available to you for people who want to be speed runners or just play the game normally without needed to play hardcore on this game.

**Core Mechanics:**

To have a running player as your character to protect is the most basic common thing from endless runner games since they only run straight, this game is no difference compare to other games. The player will move in a straight line while you control the crane using WASD to move around, Q and E to move up and down, and pressing SPACE to pick up the metal objects. It probably will also take time to try and get use to the controls but after a few tries it should be very suitable to work with since it doesn't require much of a process to know what the player is controlling.
